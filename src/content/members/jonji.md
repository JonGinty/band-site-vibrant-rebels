"Hey Murray, can we put some bouzouki on this track?" — Jonji, multi-instrumentalist and all-round musical wizard, Jonji is a master of both music and writing boring copy for a website nobody will actually read — AND THAT INCLUDES YOU!

Jonji has been making music for over a quarter of a century, having gigged on bass, guitar, alto saxophone, tenor saxophone, harmonica, mandolin, tenor banjo, 5 string banjo and vocals.

Jonji and Murray have played in bands together since 2009, having visited genres such as grunge, funk, ska, metal, hip hop, and more!

Outside of music, Jonji has had an illustrious career as the world’s greatest software engineer (soon to be replaced by AI), as well as a … less lucrative foray into streaming video games on twitch instead of eating lunch ([twitch.tv/jonji13](https://twitch.tv/jonji13)).

Jonji also built this website!
