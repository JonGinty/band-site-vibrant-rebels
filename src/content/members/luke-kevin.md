Luke Kevin is a versatile actor and musician, gaining musical influence from a wide array of genres including Jazz, Funk, Pop, Rock, Soul and more. 

Initially, he was taught by his drum teacher, Laskay Péter, who is an incredibly talented and accomplished Jazz/Funk Fusion drummer currently playing with Wagner Emil's band. Laskay Péter's drumming style and teachings have been (and continue to be) instrumental in Luke's musical journey.

Later on in life, Luke started playing and experimenting with the drums on his own as he was travelling around a lot and started to develop his own, unique style. 

Using Buddy Rich's signature Vic Firth sticks, he's become a prolific drummer and musician with hopes of being endorsed by Gretsch one day.
