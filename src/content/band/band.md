Vibrant Rebels formed in 2025 in Scotland when guitarist Murray Boyd sent multi-instrumentalist Jonji a message to the tune of "Hey, we should do another band, no assholes this time though!".

Now featuring a line-up of ten musicians, Vibrant Rebels are ready to tear up any stage they can squeeze on to, big or small, with their unique musical blend of hip-hop, pop, ska, funk and rock!
