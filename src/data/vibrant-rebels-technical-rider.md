## Backline Requirements

### Bass: Chats

#### Preferred:

- Bass amplifier on stage
- 1 x UK 13A mains socket

#### Minimum:

- 1 x UK 13A mains socket
- DI or XLR connection to venue mixing system

### Electric Guitar: Murray

#### Required:

- Guitar amplifier
- 1 x UK 13A mains socket

FOH miking of the guitar amplifier is at the engineer's discretion.

### Drums: Luke

#### Preferred house kit:

- Kick drum
- Kick pedal
- Snare drum
- 3 x toms
- Hi-hat stand
- Ride cymbal stand
- 2 x crash cymbal stands
- Drum stool

Luke will provide his own cymbals.

With at least **7 days' notice**, the band can provide:

- Snare drum
- Basic drum kit

Drum miking is at the venue engineer's discretion.

### Jonji: Acoustic Guitar

#### Signal chain:
**Acoustic guitar -> onboard piezo/preamp -> Zoom multi-FX -> venue**

Any of the following can be accommodated:

1. 1/4-inch jack into a venue DI box.
2. Direct XLR connection to the mixing system.
3. Guitar amplifier with a suitable clean channel.

Jonji can provide two XLR cables if required.

The guitar signal can be supplied either dry or with onboard effects and reverb, depending on FOH preference.

## Power Requirements

| Performer | Requirement | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chats | 1 x UK 13A socket | Bass MultiFX Unit |
| Murray | 1 x UK 13A socket | Guitar Pedal Board |
| Sparky | 1 x UK 13A socket | Laptop and Controllers / Interface |
| Christie | 1 x UK 13A socket | Keyboard |

Jonji's performance equipment does **not** require venue mains power.

Oz's wireless microphone equipment is battery powered and does not require venue mains power.

Where possible, mains supplies should be positioned close to the relevant performer to avoid cables crossing performance areas.

## Monitoring

Murray, Oz, and Sarah require stage monitoring for vocals.

Monitoring for the remaining performers is at the engineer's discretion. We do not require individual monitor mixes for every performer.

As a minimum:

- The three vocalists should have suitable vocal monitoring.
- Luke should be able to hear enough of the band to perform comfortably.
- The rest of the band should be able to hear the drums adequately.

Jonji uses a self-managed wireless IEM system and does not require a separate venue monitor mix.

The band will manage its own wireless equipment, but is happy to coordinate frequencies with the venue where required.

Jonji carries a wired backup for the acoustic guitar in the event of wireless interference or reliability problems.

## Instrument Switching

Jonji switches between tenor saxophone and acoustic guitar during the set.

The acoustic guitar is muted locally when not in use.

The saxophone microphone doesn't have a mute setting built in, but can be muted locally if routed through Jonji's personal mixer. If the saxophone is instead connected directly to FOH, the engineer may need to mute or unmute that channel during instrument changes where appropriate.

## Stage Layout

The exact stage layout is flexible and may be adapted to suit the venue.

The only strong preference is for the three vocalists, Murray, Oz, and Sarah, to occupy the central front vocal positions.

Placement of the horns, guitar, bass, keyboard, electronics and percussion, and drums may otherwise be determined by the venue engineer according to available stage space, monitoring, and backline.

Please confirm any required band-supplied backline in advance.

## General Notes

- Horn players will occasionally run out and interact with the crowd. If there is a technical (or otherwise) reason this would not be appropriate at this venue, please let us know and we'll stay in the stage area.
- Band-supplied horn wireless systems have an approximate working range of 20 m under suitable conditions.
- Please advise us in advance of any venue-specific RF restrictions or wireless coordination requirements.
- Exact input numbering and patching may be altered to suit the venue console.
- We are happy to adapt the setup in consultation with the venue engineer.
